
Here is the complete, step-by-step company onboarding flow for My Dubai Broker, covering every detail from the document:

When a new company signs up for an account, they must go through a mandatory onboarding wizard.

Step 1: Select Listing CategoriesThe very first step requires the company to select the asset categories they intend to list.This is a multi-select checkbox feature.The available options are: Real Estate, Cars, Yachts, and Private Jets / Helicopters.

Step 2: Business VerificationThe company must upload specific verification documents depending on the categories they selected in Step 1.

If Real Estate is selected:
Required: Trade License, RERA License, and BRN (Broker Registration Number).
Optional at Onboarding: Owner/Manager Emirates ID or Passport.

Note: RERA Permit numbers are optional at onboarding and are required per individual listing.

If Cars are selected:
    Required: Trade License, Car Dealer License / Showroom Registration, and Owner/Manager Emirates ID or Passport.

If Yachts are selected:
    Required: Marine Trade License, Boat Dealer Certificate, and Owner/Manager Emirates ID or Passport.

If Jets / Helicopters are selected:
    Required: Aviation Dealer License, AOC/Operator Document, and Owner/Manager Emirates ID or Passport.

Step 3: Auto-Generate Verified IDsOnce the admin panel verifies the company's uploaded documents, the system will automatically generate unique identification numbers.The system generates one unique ID per selected category for the company.

Examples of company IDs: MDB-REAL-01234, MDB-CAR-00921, MDB-YACHT-00145, MDB-JET-00023.The system also generates specific IDs for agents (e.g., MDB-AGENT-2134).

Step 4: Company Profile SetupAfter verification and ID generation, the company completes their profile setup by filling out the following fields:

Company name Description Address (This is auto-filled using the Google Maps API) Contact emails Phone numbers Website URL Social media links Company logo Cover image Listing logo Languages spoken Lead API Key (This is auto-generated by the platform) Once Step 4 is complete, the company is fully onboarded and is redirected to their main dashboard.

Crucial Logic: The dashboard and the listing fields will dynamically change based on the specific categories the company selects here.



What happens after step 3

    Option 2: The "Soft Gate" (Better User Experience)In this flow, you keep the momentum going by letting them set up their profile while the admin reviews the documents in the background.

    Action: The company completes Step 1 , Step 2 , and you immediately let them proceed to Step 4 (Company Profile Setup) to upload their logo, description, etc..

    Restricted Dashboard: Once they finish Step 4, they enter the main dashboard. However, their account status is "Pending."

    The Lock: You lock the "My Listings" tab. They cannot create a listing until the Admin officially verifies the documents and the system generates their verified category IDs.



Admin flow


Company Management & Verification
Admins and Super Admins are provided with specific tools to oversee new company sign-ups. Their responsibilities during the onboarding flow include:


Document Verification: Admins are responsible for reviewing and verifying the various business documents (like Trade Licenses and RERA Licenses) that companies upload during Step 2 of the onboarding process.


Approval or Rejection: Based on their review of the submitted documents, admins have the authority to explicitly approve or reject the company's business verification.


Category ID Assignment: Once a company's verification is approved, the admin panel is used to assign the unique, auto-generated category IDs (such as MDB-REAL-01234 or MDB-CAR-00921) to that company.
